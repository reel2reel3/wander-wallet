Step-by-Step Road Workflow

Follow this 4-step routine before, during, and after every travel shoot

STEP 1 • PRE-PRODUCTION

✈️ Set Up Your Shoot Trip

Before flying out, click + New Trip. Enter the trip name (e.g., Japan Shoot - Nov 2026), target budget in USD, number of planned YouTube episodes, and the local currency (e.g., JPY).

STEP 2 • ON THE ROAD

📸 Log Expenses & Snap Receipts

Whenever you pay for a hotel, Shinkansen ticket, or crew meal, tap + Add Expense on your phone. Pick the category, type the local amount, and tap the receipt box to snap the paper receipt with your camera.

STEP 3 • POST-PRODUCTION

🎬 Track Sponsor & AdSense ROI

As videos go live, click + Log Revenue to record mid-roll sponsor integrations, dedicated brand deals, and 30-day YouTube AdSense payouts. Wander Wallet calculates your exact ROI multiple and net profit.

STEP 4 • TAX TIME

🧾 Export Schedule C CSV & Reimburse

Filter by trip or view all trips, check for any Crew Reimbursable items paid on personal cards, and click ⬇ Export Tax CSV to hand a clean spreadsheet to your CPA.

📱 Install Wander Wallet on Your Phone

Use Wander Wallet as a full-screen mobile app with instant camera access

🍎 iPhone / iPad (iOS Safari)
  1. Open your live Railway URL (.up.railway.app) in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an upward arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Launch Wander Wallet from your home screen — it opens full-screen with a thumb-friendly bottom bar!
🤖 Android (Google Chrome)
  1. Open your live Railway URL in Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ (three dots) menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  4. Tap + Expense anytime on the road to snap receipts directly with your rear camera.
☁️ How Cross-Device Cloud Sync Works
When hosted on Railway, your desktop, laptop, and phone all read and write to the same live SQLite database (indicated by the green Cloud SQLite Synced badge in the top-left). If you lose cell service in a tunnel or flight, Wander Wallet falls back to local device cache until you reconnect.

🧾 YouTube Creator Tax & Receipt Cheat Sheet

Built-in rules to maximize your production deductions and stay audit-proof

🍜 100% On-Camera Food vs. 50% Travel Meals
Food, restaurants, or street food challenges featured on camera as the subject of a YouTube video are generally treated as 100% deductible Production / Cost of Goods Sold (COGS). Standard off-camera crew travel meals are typically subject to the 50% IRS limit. You can toggle between 100% and 50% anytime in the Tax & CSV Export Hub.
📸 The IRS $75 Receipt Rule & Hotel Folios
The IRS requires documentary receipts for any lodging expense (regardless of amount) and for any other travel/production expense of $75 USD or more. Use the Missing Receipt (>$75) filter in the Dashboard Ledger before wrapping a trip to make sure no big receipts are missing.
💳 Crew Reimbursements (Accountable Plan)
If a camera operator, editor, or host pays for a train ticket or meal on a personal card or cash, check Needs Crew Reimbursement when logging the expense. Filter by Crew Reimbursable in the ledger to see the exact USD total to reimburse them tax-free.
